Getting started

I installed Hunker but I can't find it

Hunker has no Dock icon by design — it lives in the menu bar. Look along the top of your screen for the ring icon and click it to open the timer.

If the menu bar is crowded, macOS may have hidden it. Try widening the menu bar area or quitting another menu bar app to make room.

How do I start a session?

Click the menu bar icon and press Start, or press ⌘ Return while the popover is open. Type a label in the field first if you want the session tagged with what you're working on.

Out of the box Hunker runs 25-minute focus sessions with 5-minute short breaks, and a 15-minute long break after every 4 completed focus sessions. All four are adjustable in Preferences → Timer.

Notifications and sound

I'm not getting an alert when a session ends

Check Preferences → Alerts. If it says notifications are turned off, use the button there to open System Settings and enable them for Hunker.

A Focus mode or Do Not Disturb will also suppress the alert. Hunker's notification is marked time-sensitive, so allowing time-sensitive notifications for Hunker in System Settings → Notifications lets it through.

Which sounds can I choose?

The free version includes Glass, Ping and Pop. Hunker Pro unlocks the full macOS system sound library. You can mute the completion sound entirely on either tier from Preferences → Alerts.

Your data

Where is my session history stored?

On your Mac, inside Hunker's own sandbox container. It is never uploaded anywhere — Hunker ships without the network entitlement, so macOS prevents it from connecting to the internet at all. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Does Hunker ever delete my history?

No. History is unlimited on every tier and is never pruned or throttled. If your Pro trial ends, the extended charts stop being displayed but nothing is removed — buying Pro brings the same data straight back.

Resetting your preferences from Preferences → Advanced also leaves history untouched.

Why doesn't a skipped session show in my stats?

Skipping logs the partial session so your history stays honest, but marks it incomplete. Only completed focus sessions count toward totals, streaks and the charts. You'll still see skipped sessions in the history list, marked as such.

Can I get my data out?

Hunker Pro exports your complete history as a CSV file — one row per session, with start and end time, planned and actual length, phase, completion and label. You choose where it saves; nothing is uploaded.

Hunker Pro

What does Pro add?

  • 30-day and all-time charts, current and longest streaks, all-time totals
  • The full macOS system sound library
  • Five appearance themes
  • A desktop widget showing today's focus time and your streak
  • Shortcuts and Siri support
  • CSV export of your history
  • Automatic pause when your Mac sleeps or the screen locks
  • A global keyboard shortcut, and colour-coded tags with a per-tag breakdown

Every new install gets all of it free for 7 days. After that the free tier keeps working exactly as it did, and Pro is a single one-time purchase — there is no subscription and nothing to renew.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open the paywall from the bottom of the popover, or Preferences → Hunker Pro, and press Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Can my family use it?

Yes — Hunker Pro has Family Sharing enabled.

Pro features that need setup

The widget says "Open Hunker to finish setting up"

The widget reads a small summary the app publishes. Launch Hunker at least once and complete a session, then the widget will fill in on its next refresh.

My global shortcut doesn't work

The default is ⌃⌥R. If macOS or another app has already claimed the combination, Hunker tells you so in Preferences → Advanced — record a different one there. A shortcut needs at least one of ⌘, ⌥ or ⌃, so it can't swallow ordinary typing.

Launch at login isn't sticking

macOS sometimes requires you to approve the item yourself. Check System Settings → General → Login Items and make sure Hunker is enabled there. Hunker will tell you in Preferences → Advanced when approval is needed.

Timing

What happens if my Mac sleeps mid-session?

Nothing is lost. Hunker counts down to a fixed end time rather than ticking, so display sleep, App Nap and the Mac sleeping can't make it drift. Quit and relaunch mid-session and the countdown picks up against real elapsed time; if the session finished while Hunker was closed, it's logged at the time it actually ended.

Hunker Pro can also pause automatically when the Mac sleeps or the screen locks — turn that on in Preferences → Advanced.

I changed a duration mid-session and nothing happened

That's deliberate. A running countdown is never re-timed underneath you — new durations take effect from the next session.
